Past Papers & Competition Resources

The competition sets on this site are original practice problems in the style of each contest. They are not real past-paper questions. Real papers belong to their organisers, so use these official sites for them:

  • NZQA: NCEA Mathematics and Statistics past exams and exemplars
  • NZQA: Mathematics subject page (includes NZ Scholarship Calculus and Statistics past exams)
  • International Mathematical Olympiad official archive
  • MAA: AMC 8, AMC 10, AMC 12, AIME and USAMO
  • UKMT: Junior, Intermediate and Senior Challenges and BMO
  • Australian Maths Trust: Australian Mathematics Competition
  • Canadian Mathematical Society: CMO and Canadian competitions
  • Art of Problem Solving: contest archives and discussion

Tip: attempt a paper under timed conditions, mark it with the official solutions, then add the questions you missed to your plan.

Flight Entrance Exam

Welcome, cadet. This exam walks through every topic on the site from easiest to hardest, one question each. It keeps getting harder, all the way up to IMO level, and only ends when you reach the end or make 3 mistakes (skipping counts as a mistake). Every level you reach unlocks as you go, and your progress saves after every answer so you can stop and resume later. There is no timer. No calculator needed. Enter numbers or fractions like 7/8. Your result is saved on this device.
